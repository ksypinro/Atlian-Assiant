Usage: twg confluence space get [options] [identifier]

Get a single Confluence space by identifier (space key or numeric ID); returns
full space metadata including type, status, description, homepage, labels,
enabled content types, and space instructions.

Arguments:
  identifier  Confluence space identifier (space key or numeric ID)

Options:
  --labels    Include space labels
  -h, --help  display help for command

Examples:
  $ twg confluence space get ENG --site hello
  $ twg confluence space get 1234567 --site hello
  $ twg confluence space get ENG --labels

Guards:
  Use for: resolving a known space's full metadata by key or ID.
  Don't use for: listing many spaces (use confluence space list) or page lookups (use confluence content get).

Synonyms: space detail, get space, space info, lookup space
